How the calculation works

STEP 01
Remaining lectures

Total lectures minus completed lectures.

STEP 02
Days remaining

Your chosen deadline minus today's date, with today counted as one available day.

STEP 03
Daily target

Remaining lectures divided by available days, rounded up. Recalculated whenever your data changes.

The daily mission is a recommendation, not a forced schedule. If the total workload is too high, the app flags it so you can adjust the deadline or workload.
